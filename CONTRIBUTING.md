# Contributing to AsmWeb

Thank you for considering a contribution.

The public AsmWeb source is licensed under the Mozilla Public License, Version 2.0 (`LICENSE`). Contributors should ensure that they have sufficient rights to submit their contributions under MPL-2.0 and disclose any relevant third-party source or licensing obligations.

- Do not submit copied third-party code without identifying its source and applicable licence.
- Clearly identify substantial third-party material, generated assets, or borrowed implementation fragments and their provenance.
- Do not remove copyright, author-attribution or licence notices that must be retained.
- Include relevant tests and respect the engineering specifications and qualification boundaries.
- Project maintainers may decline changes that expand scope or introduce incompatible licensing obligations.

Contributors retain their applicable copyright. Submission of a contribution does not, by itself, assign copyright to the project maintainer or grant unspecified separate proprietary relicensing rights. Any additional rights agreement, if ever required, must be explicit and independently agreed.
